# SAM Group Platform

## Project Overview

SAM Group Platform is a custom B2B web platform for the petroleum, lubricants, and petrochemical industry.

The platform is designed to be scalable, modular, secure, and maintainable for many years.

This project is NOT a simple company website.

It will gradually evolve into a complete business platform.

## Customer Communication

International customers communicate primarily via **WhatsApp**, not email or phone — this must be a first-class contact channel on the Contact Us page ("WhatsApp Us" CTA, see [SITE_STRUCTURE.md §6](./SITE_STRUCTURE.md#6-contact-us)), not an afterthought alongside email/phone.

Primary export target markets: Africa, neighboring countries, India, and Turkey (Turkey noted as regulatorily strict). Export & Logistics content and future market-expansion decisions should reflect these actual markets rather than generic global-region placeholders.

The site launches in three languages — English (default), Persian, and Arabic — covering the domestic, international-buyer, and Middle East/Africa export-market audiences respectively. Full strategy: [SITE_STRUCTURE.md](./SITE_STRUCTURE.md) and [i18n/INTERNATIONALIZATION_STRATEGY.md](./i18n/INTERNATIONALIZATION_STRATEGY.md).
