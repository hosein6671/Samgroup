# Lubricant Additives & Components — Content Sources

Status: internal editorial research record. These sources inform category-level explanatory copy only. They do not establish a SAM product formulation, treat rate, performance level, approval, compatibility statement or typical property.

## Primary sources reviewed

1. Lubrizol, **Lubricant and Fuel Additives** — confirms that lubricant-additive solutions are application-specific and span transportation and industrial uses.
   https://www.lubrizol.com/en/Lubricant-and-Fuel-Additives
2. Lubrizol, **Ready Reference Guide** — provides primary-industry context for lubricant additives, engine oils, driveline fluids, grease, hydraulic fluids and related specifications.
   https://360.lubrizol.com/Resources/Ready-Reference-Guide
3. Afton Chemical, **Automatic Transmission Fluid** — supports the conservative statement that ATF additive systems balance multiple components and are reviewed for a defined transmission-fluid application.
   https://www.aftonchemical.com/products/driveline-additives/automatic-transmission-fluid/
4. Afton Chemical, **Automotive Gear Oil Additives** — supports application-led navigation for automotive gear additive packages.
   https://www.aftonchemical.com/products/driveline-additives/automotive-gear/

## Permitted use

- Explain why additive packages are organised by finished-fluid application.
- Explain why selection proceeds from application and target specification to a product-specific technical review.
- Distinguish category guidance from a product TDS, SDS and batch COA.
- Describe the site taxonomy without publishing competitor products or claims.

## Explicit exclusions

- No competitor product name, numerical value, treat rate or claimed performance is copied to the public page.
- No API, ACEA, ILSAC, JASO, OEM, DEXRON or other approval is attributed to a SAM product without its reviewed product source.
- No compatibility, formulation, production-capacity, certification, MOQ or lead-time claim is inferred.
- Transformer oil, white oil and rubber process oil remain in the existing SAM taxonomy; the sources above do not redefine their identity or formulation.

Public pages intentionally contain no external-source links. Source provenance remains internal so buyers see the SAM product information rather than competitor references.
