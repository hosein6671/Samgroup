# Product Families — Content Sources

Status: internal editorial research record for the Automotive, Industrial, Marine and Coolant family pages. These sources support conservative selection guidance only. They do not establish a SAM formulation, approval, compatibility statement, test result or product value.

## Engine Oils & Automotive Lubricants

- American Petroleum Institute, **Engine Oil Licensing & Certification System** — establishes that engine-oil performance categories and certification marks are distinct from SAE viscosity classification and depend on formal requirements and testing.
  https://www.api.org/products-and-services/engine-oil
- American Petroleum Institute, **Motor Oil Guide** — supports directing the buyer to the vehicle manufacturer's recommendation and the required performance level rather than selecting by vehicle class alone.
  https://www.api.org/products-and-services/engine-oil/documents/motor-oil-guide

Permitted use: explain that vehicle segment is only the first filter and that viscosity grade and performance specification require product-level confirmation. No API mark or category is attributed to SAM.

## Industrial Oils & Lubricants

- Chevron Lubricants, **Industrial Products FAQs** — supports the conservative distinction between equipment/application, viscosity, water behaviour, base-fluid chemistry and compatibility review.
  https://www.chevronlubricants.com/en_us/home/learning/about-our-brands/industrial-products/industrial-products-faqs.html
- Chevron Lubricants, **Compressor Oils** — confirms that compressor type and application are material product-selection inputs.
  https://www.chevronlubricants.com/en_us/home/products/product_category/industrial_oils/compressor_oils.html

Permitted use: explain an equipment- and duty-led enquiry. Competitor products, claimed benefits, grades and approvals are not copied.

## Marine Oils & Lubricants

- MAN Energy Solutions / Everllence, **Marine Project Guides** — documents distinct engine lubricating-oil and cylinder-lubrication systems and supports asking for engine/system context before selection.
  https://www.man-es.com/applications/projectguides
- Wärtsilä, **Main engine lubricating oil system** and engine auxiliary-system material — supports treating the machinery and lubrication system as primary enquiry inputs.
  https://www.wartsila.com/encyclopedia/term/main-engine-lubricating-oil-system

Permitted use: explain system-led navigation and request engine/equipment, operating, fuel, volume and port context. No OEM approval or product recommendation is inferred.

## Antifreeze & Coolants

- BASF GLYSANTIN, **Marine and industrial coolant applications** — confirms that glycol base, inhibitor technology, concentration and engine requirements are separate technical dimensions; it also identifies OAT, hybrid and Si-OAT as distinct technologies.
  https://fuel-and-lubricants.basf.com/global/en/industries/marine
- BASF GLYSANTIN, **Coolants portfolio** — supports distinguishing coolant chemistry and powertrain/system requirements rather than selecting by colour.
  https://fuel-and-lubricants.basf.com/global/en/portfolio/coolants

Permitted use: explain chemistry-, system- and supply-form-led selection. Competitor colours, products, approvals, service intervals and performance claims are not transferred to SAM.

## Global exclusions

- Public pages carry no external-source or competitor links.
- Existing SAM product-family names and formulation scope remain unchanged.
- No numerical value, compatibility, specification, approval, certification, capacity, MOQ or lead time is introduced without reviewed SAM evidence.
- Product TDS/SDS and batch COA remain the authoritative product-level documents after their normal review gates.
