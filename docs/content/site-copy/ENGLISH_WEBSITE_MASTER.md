### Introduction

Heading: **For requirements that need a closer look.**

Body: A custom request may begin with a target specification, an operating problem, a reference product, or a packaging requirement. The form is designed to capture what is known without forcing assumptions about what is not.

### What to include in your request

- Product specification and property targets
- Application-led product selection
- Additive-package or component requirement
- Packaging and presentation requirement
- Destination and supply configuration

### Process

Heading: **A requirement-led process**

1. **Define the brief:** Share the application, specification, and operating context.
2. **Review the requirement:** Technical and commercial information is checked for completeness.
3. **Clarify the target:** Open questions, constraints, and evaluation criteria are agreed.
4. **Evaluate the option:** Available products or a proposed route are assessed against the brief.
5. **Sample and validate:** Where applicable, a sample supports evaluation before commitment.
6. **Confirm supply:** Final specification, quantity, packaging, destination, and commercial terms are documented.

### Approved general questions

**How do I find a product?**  
Browse the six product families or use the Product Finder to search by grade name, industry, application, and packaging.

**Where can I find technical documents?**  
Open the relevant product page to view published TDS and SDS files. If a document is not public, use the documentation request and name the exact product and grade.

**Can I request a sample?**  
Use the sample-request route and include the product, intended application, destination, and evaluation purpose. Availability and dispatch conditions are confirmed for the request.

**What should I include in a quotation request?**  
Include the product or grade, required quantity, packaging preference, destination, and preferred Incoterm. Add the required specification when relevant.

**What if I cannot find an exact catalogue match?**  
Submit a custom-product request with the application, required properties or specification, estimated quantity, packaging, and destination.

**Are the values on product pages guaranteed specifications?**  
Not unless the applicable product or sales document explicitly says so. Values labelled “typical” support initial evaluation and should not be treated as contractual limits.
