Who we are

A manufacturer, not a trading company.

SAM Group produces base oils, lubricants and additives, and supplies them to businesses that buy on specification. Dealing with the producer means the formulation, the packaging and the supply terms are all settled in the same conversation.

Trust indicators

What buying from the producer changes.

Four things that follow from dealing with the company that makes the product.

  • Direct manufacturer

    Your specification reaches the people who produce the product, without an intermediary in between.

  • Sample before commitment

    Engine oil and base oil enquiries begin with a sample, so the product is evaluated before anything is agreed.

  • Formulation to requirement

    Where the catalogue does not answer the need, the product is developed against the requirement instead.

  • Supply built around the order

    Bulk, flexitank, IBC and drum, selected for the volume and the route rather than offered as a fixed list.
